How It Works

Three quiet steps.

No batteries, no harsh sprays, and no disposable plastic pods. Refill in seconds whenever you want a fresh lift.

  1. 01

    Apply Oil to a Refill Stick

    Add 5 to 8 drops of FINCHLEAF essential oil directly onto an unscented refill stick. Adjust the drop count anytime to tailor intensity.

  2. 02

    Slide Into the Bar

    Seat the scented stick neatly inside the slim matte-black anodized aluminum bar behind the natural wood inlay and horizontal vent slot.

  3. 03

    Clip Onto Any Air Vent

    Press the soft silicone-coated dual-prong rear clip onto any horizontal or vertical car vent. Natural cabin airflow diffuses the scent evenly.

Care & Use Guide

Simple upkeep, clean cabin.

  • Apply directly onto the stick Place oil drops onto the refill stick over a cloth or tissue before sliding it into the bar, keeping essential oil off painted dash trim.
  • Dial in your strength Start with 5 drops for a light, fresh presence; add 2–3 extra drops after a week or two whenever you want a renewed lift.
  • Switch scents cleanly Pair each scent with its own unscented refill stick when alternating between Lemongrass and Sandalwood.
  • Wipe dry to clean Keep the anodized aluminum and natural wood finish crisp with an occasional pass of a dry microfiber cloth.
